Chronicle

Definition of record. The timeline of a repository, generated from its record, so that nobody writes history twice.

CHRONICLE — definition of record
The chronicle is a timeline generated from the record. Every heading in a record file that opens with a date becomes an event; a bracketed tag after the date names the event's kind, and an untagged heading is a plain entry. Extractors a repository registers may add events, kinds and a set of cards. The result is written to content/chronicle.json by ckit lint, compared by ckit check, rendered at /shell/chronicle.html, and never authored.

Why it matters

A repository's history is already written down, in its logs, one dated heading at a time. A separate timeline page would be a second copy of it, and second copies drift. Generating the timeline makes it an index like the catalog or the search index: it cannot disagree with the files it points at, and the gate fails if the committed copy is older than the record.

The tag is what lets the timeline be read. A year of log headings is too many to scan; the Story view shows only the tagged ones, grouped by month, with each summary (the first paragraph under the heading) as prose. The Timeline view shows everything, for the reader who wants the whole trail.

Worked example

From the bird-watching log on the record page, ckit lint writes two events:

{
  "date": "2026-05-03",
  "kind": "entry",
  "title": "first swifts over the reservoir",
  "summary": "Eleven at dusk, feeding low.",
  "href": "/shell/record.html?p=log/2026.md#2026-05-03-first-swifts-over-the-reservoir",
  "source": "log/2026.md",
  "links": []
},
{
  "date": "2026-04-12",
  "kind": "decision",
  "title": "count only birds seen, not heard",
  …
}

The decision appears in Story and Timeline; the untagged sighting only in Timeline. A heading counts when it is level two to four and reads date — title, the date as YYYY-MM-DD (optionally with a THH:MMZ time). A tag nobody declared fails loud rather than quietly becoming an entry:

log/2026.md:11: unknown chronicle tag [sighting] — one of pivot · kill · decision · lesson · instrument · result (or a kind an extractor declares)
KindConventionally marksShown in
pivota change of directionStory, Timeline
killsomething deliberately stoppedStory, Timeline
decisiona choice made, with its reasonStory, Timeline
lessonsomething learnedStory, Timeline
instrumenta change to the tools or the methodStory, Timeline
resultan outcomeStory, Timeline
entryan untagged dated headingTimeline

The six core tags, each drawn in its own hue on the chronicle page. The meanings are the conventions a repository keeps; the engine only knows the names. An extractor may declare more kinds, each with a hue and whether Story shows it.

Common trap

Keeping a history page by hand. A hub or a project page that lists "what happened when" is a second timeline, written in present tense and never updated when the log gains a correction. Say the state on the page, and link to the chronicle or to the record line for how it got there.

Related concepts

The chronicle is built from the record, checked by the gate like every generated index, and extended by chronicle extractors, one kind of extension point.
